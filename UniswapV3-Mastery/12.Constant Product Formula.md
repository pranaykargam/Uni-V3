# Uniswap V3 — Constant Product Formula

## 1. Main Formula

$$
\boxed{L^2 = xy = k}
$$

- `L` = liquidity
- `x`, `y` = virtual reserves
- `k` = constant product within a segment while liquidity remains unchanged

## 2. Constant Product Using Real Reserves

$$
\boxed{
L^2 =
\left(x_r + \frac{L}{\sqrt{P_u}}\right)
\left(y_r + L\sqrt{P_l}\right)
}
$$

- `x_r`, `y_r` = real reserves
- `P_l`, `P_u` = lower and upper price boundaries

**Memory trick:**

- Real reserves + boundary offsets = virtual reserves
- Virtual reserves multiplied together = `L²`

## 3. Why It Matters

- V2 uses one shared liquidity curve.
- V3 divides liquidity into separate price segments.
- Within a segment, virtual reserves follow the constant-product relationship.
- Real reserves change as price moves.
- The segment’s liquidity and price boundaries remain fixed.
- At a segment boundary, one real reserve can reach zero without the other becoming infinite.

## 4. Auditor Takeaway


$$
\boxed{
L^2 =
\left(x_r + \frac{L}{\sqrt{P_u}}\right)
\left(y_r + L\sqrt{P_l}\right)
}
$$

- The constant-product equation is not directly implemented as one formula in the V3 codebase.
- V3 calculates token amounts using price and liquidity math.
- **Boundary crossing matters:** active liquidity may change when a swap moves into another segment.
