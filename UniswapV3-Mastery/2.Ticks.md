# Uniswap V3 — Concentrated Liquidity and Ticks

## 1. Concentrated Liquidity

In Uniswap V2, liquidity follows the constant-product curve:

\[
xy=k
\]

In Uniswap V3, LPs choose a **lower and upper price boundary** to concentrate liquidity where they expect trading to occur.

**Example:** ETH is trading at $2,000. An LP provides liquidity between $1,800 and $2,200.

- Within the range: liquidity is active and can earn swap fees.
- Outside the range: the position becomes inactive and stops earning swap fees.

Higher active liquidity generally means lower price impact for the same trade.

## 2. What Are Ticks?

**Ticks are predefined price boundaries where liquidity positions can begin or end.**

If liquidity could change at arbitrary prices, tracking liquidity changes during swaps would be more complex and costly. Ticks organize these boundaries efficiently.

- LPs must choose valid tick boundaries.
- Tick spacing depends on the pool's fee tier.
- Smaller tick spacing allows finer price ranges.
- Larger tick spacing restricts the available boundaries.

## 3. Price vs. Ticks

The **price can move continuously between ticks**, but tick boundaries remain fixed.

Think of ticks as mileage markers on a road: a car can travel between markers without stopping at each one.

## 4. What Happens When a Tick Is Crossed?

Suppose three LPs have these positions:

| LP | Price range |
|---|---|
| Alice | $1,800–$2,200 |
| Bob | $1,900–$2,100 |
| Charlie | $2,000–$2,400 |

At $2,050, all three positions are active.

When the price crosses above $2,100, Bob's position becomes inactive, so active liquidity decreases.

When a swap crosses an initialized tick, the pool updates active liquidity and continues the swap using the new liquidity, provided liquidity is available.

**A tick crossing updates active liquidity; it does not itself deposit or withdraw tokens.**

## 5. Uniswap V2 vs. V3

| Feature | V2 | V3 |
|---|---|---|
| LP-selected price ranges | No | Yes |
| Liquidity boundaries | No LP-defined ticks | Tick-based |
| Active liquidity | Follows the V2 pool model | Depends on positions covering the current price |
| Capital efficiency | Liquidity spread across the curve | Liquidity concentrated in selected ranges |

### Key Takeaway

**Uniswap V3 uses concentrated liquidity to improve capital efficiency and ticks to manage where liquidity changes. Prices move between ticks; when a tick is crossed, active liquidity is updated.**
